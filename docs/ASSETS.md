# Assets

Reference plates live in `docs/concept/` (`city-concept.jpg`, `agent-sheet.jpg`, `fe2vi-logo-400.png`, `fe2vi-x-banner-1500x500.png`). They set the station layout, the ingot roles, and the mark. The meshes are not traced from those plates.

The station shells, the agents, and the mark are original. They are not traced from a character pack. Interior rooms keep an original shell (walls, vault door, lasers, columns) and add CC0 props from `tools/blender/dress_interiors.py`. The mark at `apps/web/public/fe2vi-logo-400.png` is also `app/icon.png` and the Open Graph image.

Rebuild from the repo root:

```sh
blender --background --python tools/blender/build_station.py
npx --yes @gltf-transform/cli@4.1.1 meshopt apps/web/public/models/station.glb apps/web/public/models/station.glb
npx --yes @gltf-transform/cli@4.1.1 meshopt apps/web/public/models/agent.glb apps/web/public/models/agent.glb
```

Blender 4.0 builds Z-up. The exporter writes glTF Y-up. `layout.json` is in glTF meters. Room markers inside `station.glb` are empties named `marker.<district-id>`.

The scene GLBs and the nine street-prop GLBs use `EXT_meshopt_compression` (plus `KHR_mesh_quantization` and `KHR_materials_emissive_strength`). Draco is not used: this Blender build has no Draco encoder, and a CDN decoder is the wrong trade. The scene loads them with three's `MeshoptDecoder`. Props were recompressed with `@gltf-transform/cli meshopt`, cutting the prop folder from 4.6 MB to 2.0 MB. The environment HDRI is resized to 512px (372 KB).

The visible sky is an original blue-hour gradient (indigo, violet, a thin orange horizon, and a few stars). The CC0 dawn HDRI is only a dim reflection source. Steel and grate images are generated by `tools/blender/make_textures.py`.

`nature.glb` mixes two CC0 packs, assembled by `tools/blender/import_nature.py`. Trees are the smoother models from Kenney's [Nature Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit), [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): tree_pineRoundA, tree_pineRoundC, tree_pineDefaultA, tree_detailed, tree_oak, tree_thin. Leaf and bark colors are shifted to lawn green and timber. Bushes, flowers, rocks, grass, and the small plant stay from the [Ultimate Nature Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatenature.html) by Quaternius (June 2019), CC0, via [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-nature-pack-1): Bush_1, BushBerries_1, Flowers, Rock_1, Rock_3, Rock_Moss_2, Grass_Short, Plant_1. Trees are kept off the roads and the building fronts, and any tree sitting between the camera and its target is hidden.

The agent clips are `idle`, `walk`, and `celebrate` on `bone.root`, `bone.body`, `bone.leg.L`, and `bone.leg.R`. The walk swing is bone-local. Confirm the axis when the city plays the clip.

Trait indexes match `crates/fe2vi-core/src/skin.rs`. The scene shows one face and the parts for the equipped skin. A default skin hides every `hat.*` and `acc.*` the seed did not pick. Body color is the `Body` material, not a separate mesh. Trails are particles, not geometry.

The hip kit is a side satchel. The body stays a boxy ingot.

`apps/web/components/CityMap.tsx` loads both files with drei `useGLTF` and three-stdlib's meshopt decoder. Draco stays off so the page does not fetch a decoder.

Sound is synthesized in `apps/web/lib/audio.ts`. There is no sample file. A district bed is noise plus a sine, placed on a panner at that room, so the room near the camera is louder. The title theme is a short sine line. Clicks, hovers, and the heist, breach, trade, rank, quest, and forge stings are oscillators. Playback starts from ENTER AS SPECTATOR, or from Unmute if the spectator skipped. Mute, volume, and Quiet are stored in `localStorage` under `fe2vi.audio.v1`. Quiet and reduced motion duck the stings.

`kits.glb` is assembled by `tools/blender/import_kits.py`. Buildings and the market awnings are [City Kit (Commercial)](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial) 2.1 by Kenney, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): building-j (bank) with detail-overhang, building-n and detail-awning (market), building-skyscraper-d (war room), building-e (forge), low-detail-building-a/b/c (construction plots). Stadium stands, floodlights, and flags are [Racing Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/racing-kit) by Kenney, CC0: grandStand, grandStandCovered, lightPostLarge, overheadLights, flagGreen, flagRed, pylon, plus radarEquipment used as the war-room roof dish. Market string lights and the forge chimney are [Holiday Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/holiday-kit) by Kenney, CC0: lights-colored, cabin-roof-chimney. Plot fences are [City Kit (Suburban)](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban) 2.0 by Kenney, CC0: fence-1x4. Scaffolding, planks, and the vault signpost are [Survival Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/survival-kit) by Kenney, CC0: structure-metal, resource-planks, signpost. The pier crane is [Overhead Crane](https://polyhaven.com/a/overhead_crane) by Poly Haven, CC0, 1k diffuse only. Credit: Kenney (www.kenney.nl) and Poly Haven. The gold VAULT board and the COMING SOON boards are original 3D text. Forge smoke is a short opacity animation. FlagTankco is not used.

Interior rooms are built by `tools/blender/dress_interiors.py`, then `tools/blender/instance_rooms.py`, then meshopt. The shell, round vault door, lasers, columns, gold bars, mannequins, and trophy are original. Props are CC0 and recolored. Repeats (bars, lockers, chairs, benches, racks, cameras) are `EXT_mesh_gpu_instancing`. One-off props that share a material are joined. Textures are WebP. Draco is not used: this Blender build has no Draco library, and the page must not fetch a decoder. Per-pack notes live in `apps/web/public/models/LICENSES/`. A credits line links to `/about`.

## Fonts

UI text loads through `next/font/google` (`Chakra Petch`, `Inter`, `JetBrains Mono`). Next downloads the subsets at build time and serves them from this site. The browser does not call Google.

Chakra Petch (600 and 700) is the display face: headings, buttons, and in-world room signs. Inter (400, 500, 600) is body and HUD copy. JetBrains Mono is only for numbers, account addresses, pins, and leaderboards.

drei `Text` cannot read the woff2 subsets Next emits. In-world signs use static TrueType files served from `apps/web/public/fonts/`. `apps/web/lib/fonts.ts` points at `ChakraPetch-Bold.ttf` and `Inter-Medium.ttf`.
